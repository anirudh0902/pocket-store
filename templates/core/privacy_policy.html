Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

At POCKET, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. We collect customer name, delivery address, phone number, and email strictly for the purposes of processing orders, dispatching shipments, and providing customer support.

2. Payment Information Security

POCKET uses a secure manual UPI verification model. We do NOT collect or store sensitive banking credentials, card numbers, or UPI PINs. When you make a UPI transaction, only the 12-digit transaction UTR/reference number is recorded for bank verification purposes.

3. How Your Information is Shared

Your delivery address and phone number are shared solely with logistics and fulfillment partners necessary to deliver your package to your doorstep. We never sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

If you have any questions regarding your account or data deletion requests, please email us at {{ site_config.support_email }}.
