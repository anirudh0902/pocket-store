Shipping & Delivery Policy

POCKET All-India Logistics

1. Delivery Timeline

Once your UPI payment is verified, orders are processed and handed over to our logistics partners within 24 to 48 business hours. Typical delivery times are 4 to 7 business days depending on your delivery PIN code.

2. Shipping Charges

We believe in transparent pricing:

  • FREE Shipping: Applicable on all orders of ₹{{ site_config.free_shipping_threshold|default:'499' }} or above.
  • Standard Shipping: A flat ₹{{ site_config.standard_shipping_charge|default:'49' }} fee applies to orders below ₹{{ site_config.free_shipping_threshold|default:'499' }}.

3. Real-Time Tracking

As soon as your shipment is dispatched, you will receive an update in your POCKET account with the courier partner name (e.g. Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC, XpressBees) and an active AWB tracking number.
