Returns & Refunds Policy

1. 7-Day Replacement Guarantee

At POCKET, we take pride in delivering genuine fashion products. If you receive an item that is defective, damaged in transit, or incorrect in size, you are eligible for a replacement or return within 7 days of delivery.

2. Eligibility Conditions

  • Item must be unused, unwashed, and in original condition with tags intact.
  • Original packaging must be preserved.
  • Photographs of any defect/damage should be shared with our support team.

3. Refund Processing

Once the return is inspected and approved by our fulfillment team, the refund amount will be credited directly to your UPI ID or bank account within 3-5 business days.
